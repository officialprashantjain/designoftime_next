Hot Ticket - Identity design for a new streetwear brand

Built at the intersection of culture and self-expression, Hot Ticket set out to create a brand that feels raw, confident, and unapologetically bold. Rooted in streetwear, the idea was to move beyond just apparel and build a visual language that people can identify with instantly.

As an early step in shaping the brand, we developed a distinct voice, visual direction, and identity system that could carry across drops, collaborations, and digital presence. The focus was on creating something flexible yet strong enough to evolve with trends while still holding a clear point of view.

The resulting identity is sharp, minimal, and high-impact. Designed to stand out in a crowded space, it commands attention while staying true to the attitude and energy of modern street culture.

Assets

We created a cohesive asset system for Hot Ticket with a bold logotype, flexible marks, strong typography, and a high-contrast visual language, ensuring consistency across packaging, digital, and all touchpoints
