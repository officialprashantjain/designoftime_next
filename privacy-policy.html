Global Privacy Statement

Design Of Time Co.

Effective Date: 1st April 2026

Design Of Time Co. ("Design Of Time", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

This Global Privacy Statement explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you:

  • visit our website
  • contact us for business, support, partnerships, or general inquiries
  • apply for a role or internship
  • interact with us through professional communications and events

By using our website or engaging with us, you agree to the practices described in this statement.

1. Who We Are

Design Of Time Co. is a creative and technology company offering services such as branding, marketing, web development, mobile app development, software solutions, consulting, and related business services.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Statement or how your personal data is handled, you may contact us at:

Email: hello@designoftime.co.in

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

a. Contact Information

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • company name
  • country or city
  • any details you include when contacting us

b. Business and Inquiry Information

  • project details
  • service interests
  • meeting notes
  • correspondence shared through forms, email, or calls

c. Recruitment Information

  • name
  • phone number
  • email address
  • resume or CV
  • portfolio links
  • work history
  • education details
  • any information submitted as part of a job or internship application

d. Technical and Usage Data

  • IP address
  • browser type
  • device type
  • operating system
  • pages visited
  • time spent on the website
  • referral source
  • cookie and analytics data

e. Communication Preferences

  • whether you want to hear from us
  • newsletter or marketing preferences
  • preferences shared during your interactions with us

3. How We Collect Your Data

We may collect personal data when:

  • you fill out a contact or inquiry form
  • you email or call us
  • you apply for a job or internship
  • you subscribe to updates or newsletters
  • you interact with our website through cookies or analytics tools
  • you communicate with us during meetings, pitches, or events

4. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data only where necessary and for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • respond to inquiries and requests
  • discuss potential projects or partnerships
  • provide our services
  • manage client and business relationships
  • review job and internship applications
  • improve our website, services, and communication
  • send updates, marketing, or event-related communication where permitted
  • maintain website security and prevent misuse
  • comply with legal or regulatory obligations

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal data on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • your consent
  • performance of a contract
  • steps requested before entering into a contract
  • compliance with legal obligations
  • our legitimate business interests, provided your rights and interests do not override them

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand usage, and enhance user experience.

These may include:

  • essential cookies required for the website to function
  • analytics cookies to understand traffic and performance
  • preference cookies to remember settings

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that some website features may not work properly if certain cookies are disabled.

7. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share your personal data only when necessary with:

  • employees, team members, or affiliated personnel working on your request or project
  • service providers who help us operate our website, communications, recruitment, analytics, or business systems
  • legal, regulatory, or law enforcement authorities where required by law
  • professional advisors such as accountants, lawyers, or consultants where necessary

Any third party handling data on our behalf is expected to use it only for legitimate business purposes and with appropriate safeguards.

8. International Data Transfers

Because we may work with clients, collaborators, platforms, and service providers across different countries, your personal data may be processed outside your country of residence.

Where such transfers happen, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data is handled securely and in line with applicable privacy standards.

9. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including for:

  • responding to inquiries
  • delivering services
  • evaluating applications
  • maintaining records
  • meeting legal, accounting, or compliance obligations

When the data is no longer needed, we will delete, anonymize, or securely archive it as appropriate.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, disclosure, or alteration.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights including:

  • the right to access your personal data
  • the right to correct inaccurate data
  • the right to request deletion of your data
  • the right to restrict or object to certain processing
  • the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • the right to request a copy of your data, where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:

Email: hello@designoftime.co.in

We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate legal basis or consent. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

14. Changes to This Privacy Statement

We may update this Privacy Statement from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website, services, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
