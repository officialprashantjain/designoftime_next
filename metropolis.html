01 — Executive Summary

Metropolis has 40 years of trust. Its digital experience is spending it.

With 175+ labs, 220+ cities, and 50M+ tests performed, Metropolis has clinical credibility few can match. But the digital journey — from the moment a user lands on the homepage to the moment they download their report — is riddled with friction that converts trust into abandonment.

4K+ Tests offered — none surfaced smartly
4.1★ App rating — but booking failures reported
3+ Contact numbers on a single page
0 Guided entry paths for non-expert users

What We Found

Across the full digital journey — homepage, search, booking, post-booking, and report delivery — the experience completes transactions but does not build relationships. Users finish tasks in spite of the platform, not because of it. Critical issues span data sync failures, payment confirmation gaps, no guided discovery, and a fragmented support structure.

Why It Matters

Metropolis competes with Tata 1mg, PharmEasy, and Dr. Lal PathLabs — all investing heavily in AI search, guided booking, and retention loops. Every friction point costs Metropolis twice: once when a user abandons, and again when they don't return. In a category built on repeat usage — chronic care, family diagnostics, annual checks — this is a revenue retention problem, not just a UX one.

What To Do

Redesign the end-to-end journey in four phases: fix the most critical breakpoints first, then build competitive features, then add intelligence — so every stage feels connected, guided, and worth returning to.

What It Unlocks

Higher first-time conversion. Stronger repeat booking rates. Reduced inbound support volume. Cross-platform consistency. And a digital experience that matches the standard Metropolis already holds in its labs.

02 — Journey Stage Overview

Where the Metropolis journey breaks

Issues mapped across six journey stages. Every one of these has a direct cost — in drop-offs, support calls, or users who simply don't return.

4 issues

Homepage & Entry

  • City defaults to Mumbai with no auto-detection
  • Generic search bar — no guided entry for symptom, prescription, or package intent
  • Three different phone numbers visible simultaneously, causing choice paralysis
  • No personalisation or returning-user recognition
5 issues

Search & Test Discovery

  • Search is keyword-only — no AI, no symptom-to-test mapping
  • 4000+ tests with no smart filtering or comparison tool
  • Test pages lack contextual guidance (why this test, who needs it)
  • No clear pathway for users without a doctor's prescription
  • Package recommendations only appear post-search — not at entry
4 issues

Booking & Payment

  • UPI payments completing but bookings not reflecting in profile (confirmed in user reviews)
  • Location not auto-detected — users must manually select city in form
  • No option to edit or modify a booking post-confirmation
  • Slot selection UX is unclear — no visual calendar or guidance on best times
3 issues

Post-Booking State

  • No clear "what happens next" communication after confirming a booking
  • Sample tracking requires login — adds friction for anxious first-time users
  • No push notification or proactive status updates on web
4 issues

Report Access & Delivery

  • Smart reports exist but users note they need significant improvement
  • WhatsApp reports are password-protected with no in-context explanation of the password
  • No post-report guidance — results delivered without next-step recommendations
  • Reports not treated as a retention moment — no follow-up or re-booking nudge
3 issues

Web vs. App Sync

  • Addresses, bookings, and reports not synced between website and app (confirmed by users)
  • TruHealth app and main Metropolis app serve overlapping purposes with no clear distinction
  • Support flow bounces users between multiple numbers and teams — no unified escalation path

03 — Critical Issues

The five breakpoints that cost Metropolis the most

Prioritised by frequency of occurrence, user impact, and ease of resolution.

01 — Critical
Payment succeeds, booking disappears

Users completing UPI payments through GPay find no record of their booking on their profile. No time slot, no confirmation, no status. They are forced to call customer support — which itself routes them to another number.

Critical
Current Process

Payment deducted via UPI → no booking confirmation reflected in profile → user calls support → support routes to second number → user seeks refund instead of rebooking.

Expected Behaviour

Instant confirmation with booking ID, slot details, and phlebotomist assignment. Fallback SMS/WhatsApp if app state fails. A zero-gap payment-to-confirmation flow.

02 — Critical
Data lives in silos — app and website don't talk

Bookings made on the app don't appear on the website and vice versa. Addresses saved in one place don't carry over. Reports available on app may not be accessible on web. Users are forced to maintain two parallel identities.

Critical
Current Process

App bookings not visible on website. Addresses, reports, and booking history not synchronised. Users must re-enter details every time they switch platforms.

Expected Behaviour

Single unified user profile. All data — bookings, addresses, family members, reports — accessible consistently across app and web. No duplication of effort for the user.

03 — High
No guided discovery — the homepage assumes expertise

The homepage presents a search bar with no context for users who don't know test names. Someone searching "I feel tired all the time" or "my doctor said check thyroid" has no structured path. The mega-nav has five categories and dozens of items but no decision path.

High Priority
Current Process

Homepage has a search bar and category nav. Users who don't know test names must browse through Health Conditions or Health Risks — long lists with no guidance on which applies to them.

Expected Behaviour

Guided entry based on user intent: "I have a prescription," "I have a symptom," "I want a health check." Each path routes to relevant tests with smart defaults and recommendations.

04 — High
Three phone numbers, zero clarity on support

The header simultaneously shows a WhatsApp number (8422-801-801), two separate home visit booking numbers (9982-782-555 and 8104-983-787), plus a Contact Us link. Users in distress are forced to figure out which channel to use — and often get routed to yet another number.

High Priority
Current Process

Multiple contact CTAs visible simultaneously. Users report calling one number and being redirected to another. Support experience compounds the platform friction.

Expected Behaviour

One primary contact CTA contextual to the user's journey state. Post-booking: track your phlebotomist. Pre-booking: chat to book. A support experience that feels as professional as the lab.

05 — High
Reports delivered, relationship dropped

When a report is ready, Metropolis delivers it and stops. There is no contextual explanation of results, no follow-up test recommendation, no rebooking prompt, and no guidance on what a user should do next. The highest-intent moment in the journey is treated as the end of the journey.

High Priority
Current Process

Report delivered via app, email, and password-protected WhatsApp PDF. Smart report exists but users say it needs improvement. No post-report engagement or next-step guidance.

Expected Behaviour

Reports as a retention moment. Contextual "what this means," recommended follow-up tests, next annual check reminder, and one-tap rebooking for chronic patients. Turn delivery into re-engagement.

05 — Strategic Opportunities

What a better Metropolis experience looks like

Six redesign directions that collectively transform the digital experience from transaction-processor to relationship-builder.

01

Guided Discovery Flow

Replace the generic search bar with a structured intent-based entry: prescription upload, symptom exploration, or package browsing. Routes users to the right tests without requiring them to know test names — reducing zero-result searches and early drop-offs.

02

Payment-to-Confirmation Trust Loop

A zero-gap booking confirmation system. Payment triggers instant booking ID, slot confirmation, and phlebotomist assignment across all channels (in-app, SMS, WhatsApp). No user should ever not know whether their booking succeeded.

03

Unified Cross-Platform Profile

One data layer powering web and app equally. Bookings, saved addresses, family members, and report history accessible anywhere, any time. Eliminating the silo removes the single biggest source of user confusion and support calls.

04

Smart Post-Booking Status Layer

After booking, users enter a clear status timeline: confirmed → phlebotomist assigned → sample collected → processing → report ready. Proactive push and WhatsApp updates at each stage remove anxiety and eliminate "where is my report" support calls.

05

Report as a Retention Moment

Transform report delivery into a re-engagement touchpoint. Each report surfaces: what the result means in plain language, recommended follow-up tests, a reminder for the next annual check, and one-tap rebooking for chronic patients. The report is the start of the next journey.

06

AI-Powered Search & Recommendations

Symptom-to-test mapping, search history personalization, and proactive package suggestions based on prior test results and family profiles. Metropolis has the test depth. AI makes it navigable — and makes users feel known.
